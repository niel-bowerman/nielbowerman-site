Selected academic publications

Nature Climate Change · 2013

The role of short-lived climate pollutants in meeting temperature goals

Bowerman, Frame, Huntingford, Lowe, Smith & Allen

Current emissions of methane, black carbon and other short-lived climate pollutants do little to contribute to peak warming. We explore the role of these gases in meeting temperature targets.

Nature Climate Change · 2012

Equivalence of greenhouse-gas emissions for peak temperature limits

Smith, Lowe, Bowerman, Gohar, Huntingford & Allen

Peak warming can be estimated from two quantities: cumulative emissions of long-lived greenhouse gases, plus the rate of emissions of short-lived ones. We discuss what this means for setting emission targets.

Phil. Trans. Royal Society A · 2011

Cumulative carbon emissions, emissions floors and short-term rates of warming: implications for policy

Bowerman, Frame, Huntingford, Lowe & Allen

Total carbon burned controls total warming, but the rate of CO₂-induced warming is controlled by the rate of emissions. We explore the implications of this, and of emissions floors, for setting targets.

DPhil thesis · University of Oxford

Emission targets for avoiding dangerous climate change

The options for setting emissions targets to tackle climate change, the scientific case for each, and some new options that more predictably constrain warming.
